The difficulty of booking someone you cannot check

These are not customer quotes. They describe the position a person is in when a horse must be moved and the only names available have come from strangers.

A horse must be moved within the week, and the only name available belongs to a stranger nobody can vouch for.

Recommendations arrive as names alone. Calls go unanswered, and there is no way to establish whether a transporter is legal and insured.

The worry is seldom the price. It is the prospect of choosing badly and having to explain that choice afterwards.

A professional-looking advertisement is not enough.

The cost of booking transport the old way

What happens when you book

  1. You enter your postcodes, your travel date and the number of horses travelling.
  2. A price is returned in real time, before you speak to anyone.
  3. A hold is placed on your card at booking.
  4. Your job is offered to HOZGO Approved transporters.
  5. You accept the price, or choose from the offers that come back.
  6. The contract is signed in the app.
  7. You can follow your transporter live through the journey, with updates as it progresses.
  8. Your booking status and documents are kept in one place, before and after the journey.

What HOZGO Approved means

  • Every approved business completed the full process before joining.
  • All documentation is checked and legal compliance is verified.
  • HOZGO holds those documents and provides them on request.

The price you see is the price you accept, and nothing is added at checkout.

Your card is held at booking and charged on the day of the trip. The transporter is paid on completion.

If no approved transporter accepts your job, the hold is released and nothing is taken.

HOZGO does not guarantee an arrival time, and does not insure your horse or the lorry. That cover rests with the transporter, under their own signed terms.
