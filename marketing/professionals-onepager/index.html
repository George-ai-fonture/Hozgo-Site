How it works

Four steps. One app. No call-backs.

  1. R

    Route

    Enter your collection and delivery postcodes, your date and how many horses are travelling.

  2. A

    Amount

    Your price comes back in real time. The price you see is the price you accept.

  3. M

    Money held

    A hold is placed on your card. It is charged on the day of the trip, not before. If no approved transporter takes the job, the hold is released.

  4. P

    Picked up

    HOZGO Approved transporters see the job. The first to accept at your price takes it. If none can, they send offers and you choose. The contract is signed in the app and your horse travels.

HOZGO Approved

Six checks before anyone is offered your horse.

Every transporter sends us these documents before they can see a job. We check them ourselves. That is what HOZGO Approved means.

  1. Type 2 Transporter Authorisation
  2. Driver licence
  3. Vehicle registration
  4. Lorry insurance
  5. Public liability insurance
  6. Care, custody and control insurance

The transporter is paid on completion, not before. Our own lorries see every job at the same time as everyone else's.
