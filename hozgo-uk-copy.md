## How it works

howEyebrow: HOW IT WORKS
howTitle: Four steps. One app.

steps:
01 ROUTE | Enter your collection and delivery postcodes, your date and how many horses are travelling.
02 AMOUNT | Your price comes back in real time. The price you see is the price you accept.
03 MONEY HELD | A hold is placed on your card. It is charged on the day of the trip, not before.
04 PICKED UP | Your job goes to HOZGO Approved transporters. The first to accept at your price takes it. If none can, they send you offers and you choose. The contract is signed in the app, and your horse travels.
