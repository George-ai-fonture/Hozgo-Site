# HOZGO, the offer

16 September 2026
The share below was a recommendation. George agreed it on 17 September.

## Built around

GUESSED: built around believability. Every transporter promises the same dream outcome. HOZGO already wins effort and time, a price now not names by morning.
GUESSED: believability is the side she cannot fix at any price. She cannot check a lorry and her card is held before anyone accepts, so the offer hands her someone else's check.

## Name

GUESSED: The Six Checks. She can say it to a friend, "the one that checks them six ways before they touch your horse".

## Promise

HEARD, George 17 Sep, shaped by the strategist: your job is only offered to transporters who have passed six checks, Type 2 Transporter Authorisation, driver licence, vehicle registration, lorry insurance, public liability and care, custody and control insurance, and your card is held, not charged, until the day of the trip.

## The method

GUESSED: RAMP. Every horse owner knows the ramp, and what waits at the top.
HEARD, Granola 5 Sep: R, Route. Two postcodes and a date. That is the form.
HEARD, George 16 Sep: A, Amount. The price comes back in real time.
HEARD, George 17 Sep: M, Money held. A hold on her card at booking, charged on the day of the trip, he is paid on completion.
HEARD, George 17 Sep: P, Picked up. Only verified transporters see the job, one accepts first come first served or they send offers and she chooses, contract signed, horse travels.
GUESSED: P is the stretch, four stages in one. Second option, RAMPS. P, Passed, only those past the six checks see the job. S, Signed, contract then payment.
