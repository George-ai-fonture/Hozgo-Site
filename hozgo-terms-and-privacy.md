### Who Can See Live Tracking?

Where live tracking is enabled, relevant journey location information may be made available to the customer associated with that specific booking.

Customers should not receive unrestricted access to a transporter's location outside the relevant transport booking.

### Transporter Control

Live tracking is an optional feature and can be activated by the transporter where they wish to make it available for a journey.

Live tracking should cease when the relevant journey or tracking session ends.

HOZGO does not intend to continuously monitor a transporter's location when they are not undertaking a journey for which live tracking has been activated.

Any location information retained after a journey will only be retained where reasonably necessary for purposes such as security, resolving a booking dispute, investigating an incident, preventing fraud or complying with a legal obligation.

Users can manage device-level location permissions through their mobile device settings.
