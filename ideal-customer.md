## The person

Her day has nothing to do with lorries: work, the yard either side of it, rugs, feed, a phone checked in the car park. The problem lands on a date she did not choose: a vet trip, a sale, a race, new stabling, no way to drive him there herself. She asks the yard who they use, posts in a Facebook group at night, wakes to names tagged in by strangers. She rings a couple. One never rings back. The cheap one worries her and she books him anyway, in Messenger. In the kitchen she says what she left out of the group: she has paid a man off Facebook and knows nothing about him. She will not say out loud that she chose him on a photograph of a clean lorry, or that she never asked about insurance because it felt rude. The horse cannot check anyone, so she is the only check, and not qualified. To pay HOZGO she has to believe someone has already asked what she cannot ask. She will not download an app for something this rare. She will message whoever her friend used. If HOZGO shows her nobody in her area she is back in the group by lunchtime and gone for good. When it goes right, what she says afterwards is never about the lorry, but that he was professional, punctual and genuinely cared.
